Romantic

[noun] pronounced: with a candle in hand
  1. a lover of gothic aesthetics, all things dark and horror.
  2. a person who draws faces, reads books and gets lost in fictional worlds.
  3. (figurative) one who is fascinated by languages, human nature and the beauty in darkness.

I believe the most honest beauty is the kind that survives the night. A romantic soul with a curious mind, with a soft spot for red roses, lilies, the moon and the night.

The Observer

I am a serious person. I watch how people pause before they speak, what they laugh at, and what they never mention.

Every face is a locked room, and I am patient with doors.

  • The pause says more than the sentence.
  • Silence is also a confession.
  • People hide best in plain sight.

Verses in the dark

Rose

A red rose in a black room
does not ask for the sun.
It learns to bloom by candlelight
and keeps its thorns for company.

Lily

The lilies whiten in the dark
as if the night had told them something
too gentle for the day.

Moon

The moon keeps every secret
I have whispered to the dark,
and never once
has she repeated them.

Faces

Every face is a letter
sealed in wax and sorrow.
I read them slowly,
the way one reads the dead.

Under the moon

I come alive after dark. The world goes quiet, the moon takes the sky, and my thoughts finally speak up.

The night has never frightened me. It is the only hour that tells the truth.

Solitude

I love solitude. It is where I hear myself think, and where the night feels like it belongs to me.

But I wouldn’t mind meeting someone in the darkness: a stranger who keeps their own candle lit and reads the pauses between words.

If you find me in the dark,
do not bring a lantern.
Bring a quiet voice
and a story worth the silence.
